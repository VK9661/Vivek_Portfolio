# Portfolio redesign brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Direction

- Audience: people reviewing the portfolio for design work.
- Purpose: make the projects, their thinking, and the finished craft easy to understand.
- Vibe: expressive, confident, editorial, tactile, clear.
- Journey: introduction, selected work, wider project range, capabilities, contact.
- Visual structure: distinct chapters rather than one continuous world.
- Existing assets: project covers, case-study boards, motion clips, portrait and local fonts.
- Main action: explore the work; contact remains accessible throughout.
- Assumption: retain the verified project facts and existing routes while revisiting the visual identity.

## Feeling curve

1. Curiosity: a confident introduction with layered artwork.
2. Focus: a quiet transition frames the work.
3. Discovery: a featured project opens into its constituent visual layers. This is the peak.
4. Confidence: a readable collection shows the breadth of the work.
5. Connection: a simple closing invitation.

## Peak and signature

It's the site where a project cover opens into the work behind it. This bespoke transition should make the case study more inviting while leaving normal links available.

## Constraints

Keep text selectable, motion purposeful and native reading order intact. Respect reduced motion and test phone layouts, keyboard access, performance and both themes. Do not invent project facts or results. A public deployment requires Vivek's approval after review.
