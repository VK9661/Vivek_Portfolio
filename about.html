What goes into the work

four habits I keep, project after project

01

Research before reaching for solutions

Talk to users, study the market and frame the problem first.

02

Structure before pixels

Define goals and scope, then sketch flows and test the logic before any polish.

03

Craft the feel

Type, colour, hierarchy and motion: the layer that makes it feel right.

04

Prototype, test, iterate
